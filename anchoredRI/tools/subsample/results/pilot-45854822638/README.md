# Task H timing pilot at N = 50,000

The remaining 1,420 datasets are projected to take **120.75 occupied runner-hours, 111.27 CPU-hours and 6.12 hours elapsed** at 20 concurrent GitHub-hosted runners. The estimate uses 20 completed pilot datasets, one per analyte and pathology stratum. It includes two sets of 50 draws under both caps and one full-data weighted fit under both caps. The pilot stopped after these 20. [Paired results, phase times and projection](pilot/REPORT.md) · [Published roster and runner configuration](../../PILOT-CONFIG.md)

Including verification and the pilot, the projected total is **123.23 runner-hours and at least 113.19 CPU-hours**. One job processes one dataset on one numerical thread. Each stratum has 71 remaining datasets. The projection allows 24.6 seconds of overhead per job and assumes immediate dispatch as each runner becomes free. The final reporting allowance uses the measured pilot report time after removing its one-time wait. Transfers and scoring may take longer for the full cohort. ## Verification

All 848 fixture and reproducibility comparisons passed. The public and study fits agreed on identical inputs. The direct per-observation checks covered the likelihood and BIC for each transform and component count. The largest absolute discrepancy for Arm W was 1.82 × 10⁻¹¹, and its analytic one-component Gaussian mean and SD agreed within 2.21 × 10⁻¹⁰. Both unit-weight caps reproduced ordinary fitting with BIC sample size n + 40. Reusing the first three candidates reproduced the independent cap-3 search. The fixtures also passed for the two medians, zero passing fits, unresolved votes, the 25/26 boundary, upper-only CRP and lookup of a withheld full-package result. Two invocations on the same host gave byte-identical scientific records and draw positions. A third invocation ran on a separate host. Selections and decisions agreed, with maximum endpoint difference zero reference-interval widths and maximum p-value difference zero. The allowed differences were 0.001 widths and 0.0001, respectively. [Host identities and maxima](fixtures/summary.json)

## Agreement with archived Task E fits

The original study seed passed all prespecified comparisons on **1,354 of 1,440 datasets (94.0%)**. Every original exception was rerun using its archived seed. Of the 86 exceptions, 37 resolved and 49 persisted. The selected component count agreed throughout. Release decisions differed on 11 original fits and on five archived-seed reruns. All five reruns selected three components. LACT 2621 and IgE 4339 and 4371 changed from an archived decline to a release. TSH 3766 and 3768 changed from an archived release to a decline. [All 86 exceptions and their dispositions](archive/AUDIT.md) · [Original values](archive/exceptions.csv) · [Archived-seed values](archive/seed-repeats.csv)

For CRP 5026 the archived and new raw upper limits were 0.0286 and 6.5442, a difference of 227.8 archived one-sided interval widths. Both fits declined. The IgE 4371 component-1 share moved from about 0.7% to 97.4%. Twenty-nine of the 49 persistent exceptions missed only the p-value tolerance. The saved fits show that some differences persisted with identical inputs and seeds. Task E and Task H used different runtime builds, and these comparisons cannot isolate the cause. ## Pilot observations

All 80 primary medians used 50 of 50 raw fits. Six of the 80 passing-only medians had zero contributors. The six were both seed sets for CRP 4968 under cap 3, CRP 5017 under the standard cap, and LDH 5510 under cap 3. The standard majority released 19 of 20 intervals with either seed set. Cap 3 released 17 with A and 18 with B. Its only decision change was LACT 2734, with 27 declines in A and 19 in B. [Medians, shares and votes](pilot/medians.csv) · [Seed movement](pilot/seed-movement/seed-z.csv)

Six full-data standard fits invoked the fallback. Five fallbacks released. LDH 5510 was withheld. Its raw lower limit was 60.97 U/L against a true 135 U/L, giving |z| = 52.25. The standard seed-A median was 135.60 U/L and standard W gave 131.20 U/L. Neither W nor any available subsample median had an extreme endpoint under either cap. [Saved endpoint scores](pilot/pilot-scores.csv) · [Per-dataset paired results](pilot/paired-index.csv)

Independent readback reconciled 4,080 fit rows, 2,000 saved draws with 10 million positions, 160 medians and 960 decision rows. The largest p-value difference on recomputation was 1.59 × 10⁻¹⁴. All fit rows had a safeguard decision. The selected optimizers recorded 240 iteration-limit exits and eight line-search failures. Those eight came from four Hb 369 draws selected under both caps. Each passed both safeguards. These optimizer flags are retained in the per-fit export. [Independent readback](pilot/readback/verified-datasets.csv) · [Observations](pilot/readback/observations.csv) · [Per-fit diagnostics](pilot/fits.csv)

Two of 4,000 individual subsample fit rows had an endpoint with |z| > 5. Both came from standard-cap LDH 5510 in seed set A, draws 8 and 40. Their lower limits were 74.03 and 73.83 U/L. The anchor likelihood-ratio check declined both. None of the 3,571 passing subsample fits had an extreme endpoint. [Draw-level extreme results](pilot/draw-scores/DRAW-SCORES.md)

## Frozen inputs and timing recovery

The first environment build stopped before fitting on a version-string comparison. The corrected build verified installed DESCRIPTION versions and all frozen input hashes. The original fixture and audit jobs completed before the pilot. Six pilot jobs then stopped at the end of the public fallback because its RNG cleanup replaced the study timer’s exit hook. Those jobs had saved zero subsamples. The correction measures from fallback entry through the public return, including final assignments to the reporting fields. The six retries used the original compiled runtime, data and seeds. Fourteen completed original pilot jobs supplied the other records. The protocol records both corrections before their implementation.
