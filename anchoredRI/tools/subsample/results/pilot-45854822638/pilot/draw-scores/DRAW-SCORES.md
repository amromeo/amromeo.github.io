# Individual subsample intervals in the timing pilot

Of 4,000 cap-specific first-stage intervals from 2,000 distinct subsamples, two had a requested endpoint with |z| > 5. Both came from LDH Index 5510, cap 5, seed set A. Draws 8 and 40 estimated lower limits of 74.03 and 73.83 U/L against the true 135 U/L. RIbench’s support correction assigned both the same signed lower-limit deviation of +52.251. The anchor likelihood-ratio check declined both fits, with p = 0.000273 and p = 0.000118. No released subsample fit had an extreme endpoint.
